# MOJO pilot acceptance

## Before showing
- Real owner enters via Telegram, with no demonstration role selector.
- Two source-grounded draft courses; manager previews, edits, approves.
- Invite a consenting pilot employee; verify one-use invitation and own role.
- Assign lesson and confirm personal bot notification.
- Employee reads, acknowledges, answers, gets saved score.
- Manager sees attempts; passing closes further submissions.
- Review with answer explanations is available after passing or exhausting attempts.
- Restart API and verify results persist. Restore a backup into a separate disposable database.

## Pilot: up to 20 employees
- Alina validates source version and approves course content.
- Finalize trainee programme and dates of in-person menu / virtual-table assessment.
- Collect feedback on unclear questions, time spent, completion and practical performance.
- Prepare November recertification as separate approved course versions.

## After pilot
- File upload and extraction, AI drafts with source evidence and mandatory human review.
- Audio/chapter format; events and attendance; QR invitations.
- Additional manager provisioning, account lifecycle, external backup and recovery drill.
